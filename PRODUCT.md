# FCGallery

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js static export and React, TypeScript, Cloudflare Workers Static Assets only. This stack follows the gamesite-launch-flow selected by the user; no server-side player-account integration.

## Users

English-speaking EA SPORTS FC 27 players planning Gallery progress while using the game or companion marketplace. Mobile and desktop are both necessary.

## Product Purpose

Turn the user's existing Gallery scores and candidate upgrades into a concrete next route. Compare point gains, token gains, unique missing cards, trading actions, upfront coins and eventual resale loss. Explain a set-score difference without presenting assumptions as game measurements.

## Positioning

The user approved a narrow Gallery planning and score-diagnosis site after competitor research. The product focuses on level progress and fewer operations, rather than reproducing a player database or ordinary token-cost calculator. Advantage over competitors remains a hypothesis to check with task-based review.

## Operating Context

Gallery set grades, overall level and reward tokens are separate values. A card can contribute to more than one eligible set. Plans are entered from the user's game and prices; examples are explicitly illustrative. No automatic market prices or account connection are promised.

## Capabilities and Constraints

First launch: level/point route planning, optional token target, shared-card deduplication, cost versus operation comparison, import/export and shareable plan, set-score check using explicit item scores and matched bonus tags, concise guides and legal/source pages. SBC allocation is deferred as agreed. Source data and formulas must be traceable. Inputs need bounds and recoverable validation; corrupted saved/shared state must not break the page.

## Evidence on Hand

../futgg/RESEARCH.md, ../futgg/_research/build-facts.md and build-data.json, official Gallery hub/Launch Update, tool screenshots and keyword records. No player-authorized game screenshots or gameplay fixtures are available. Do not invent testimonials, price feeds, population data, or gameplay validation.

## Product Principles

- Player actions and useful results lead the page.
- Compare only the routes and prices entered; no unsupported optimal-market claim.
- Preserve useful progress, with deliberate reset and export.
- Explain grade, level points and tokens distinctly.
- Keep source-process narration in the ledger or /sources.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard controls, persistent form labels, visible focus, understandable errors, touch targets, responsive tables and light/dark themes. Calculated status should be available without relying on color.
